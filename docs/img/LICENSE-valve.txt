sc-front.svg, sc-back.svg and sc-top.svg are derived from "steam_controller_reference_20260429.pdf"
(Steam Controller reference drawing) published by Valve Corporation.

Changes: the front view (sheet 1), the underside view and the top view (sheet 2)
were cut out and converted to SVG. Removed: the sheet frame, title block,
dimensions, arrows, keep-out areas, notes, engraved legends, bolts, the thin
surface lines and duplicate rim contours. The shoulders of the underside view
and the bumpers of the top view were redrawn as single smooth contours; the
right half of the top view is the mirrored left half.
